Before you make your plan

What can I include in my plan?

Content review books, UWorld and AAMC question practice, the Khan Academy document, Anki, daily CARS, full-length exams, and any resource you add yourself.

What if everything won’t fit?

StudyCat flags the work that exceeds your available hours so you can change your workload, daily time, rest days, or test date.

Where is my plan saved?

Your plan and progress are saved to your account, so they follow you to any device.

Can I use Princeton Review instead of Kaplan?

Yes. Choose Kaplan, Princeton Review, or no content review books, and the chapter list follows your choice.
